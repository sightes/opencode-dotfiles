## Clinical Syndromes Approach

### Fever of Unknown Origin (FUO)
**Definition**: Fever >38.3°C for >3 weeks, diagnosis unclear after 1 week of investigation

**Differential (classic categories):**
1. **Infections** (30%): TB, endocarditis, abscesses, osteomyelitis
2. **Neoplasms** (20%): Lymphoma, leukemia, renal cell carcinoma, hepatoma
3. **Autoimmune** (20%): Adult Still disease, vasculitis, SLE, Crohn
4. **Miscellaneous** (15%): Drug fever, DVT, thyrotoxicosis, pheochromocytoma
5. **Undiagnosed** (15%)

**Workup:**
- CBC with differential, CMP, ESR/CRP, LDH
- Blood cultures x3 (before antibiotics)
- Urinalysis and culture
- Chest X-ray
- HIV, Hepatitis serologies
- TB testing
- Echocardiogram if murmur/endocarditis suspected
- CT chest/abdomen/pelvis if no diagnosis

### Sepsis & Septic Shock
**Sepsis-3 Definition**: Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by dysregulated host response to infection (SOFA ≥2 points)
**Septic Shock**: Sepsis with persistent hypotension requiring vasopressors + lactate >2 mmol/L

**Initial Management (Hour-1 Bundle):**
1. Measure lactate (remeasure if >2)
2. Blood cultures BEFORE antibiotics (don't delay >45 min)
3. Broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour
4. 30ml/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥4
5. Vasopressors if MAP <65 after fluids

**Empiric Antibiotics by Source:**
- **Community-acquired pneumonia (CAP)**: Ceftriaxone + Azithromycin
- **Intra-abdominal**: Piperacillin-tazobactam OR Ceftriaxone + Metronidazole
- **Skin/soft tissue**: Vancomycin + Piperacillin-tazobactam (severe) OR Cefazolin (mild-moderate)
- **Meningitis**: Vancomycin + Ceftriaxone + Ampicillin (listeria if >50yr)
- **UTI/Pyelonephritis**: Ceftriaxone (complicated) OR Nitrofurantoin (uncomplicated)

### Central Nervous System Infections

**Meningitis Bacterial:**
- Empiric: Vancomycin + Ceftriaxone (± Ampicillin if >50yr or immunocompromised)
- Add dexamethasone 10-20 min BEFORE antibiotics if pneumococcal suspected
- Duration: 7 days (N. meningitidis), 10-14 days (H. influenzae), 10-14 days (S. pneumoniae), 21 days (L. monocytogenes)

**Encephalitis (HSV most common sporadic):**
- Empiric: Acyclovir 10mg/kg IV q8h
- Also consider: autoimmune encephalitis (IVIG/steroids if negative infectious workup)

### Respiratory Infections

**Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CURB-65):**
- Confusion, Urea >7mmol/L, RR ≥30, BP (SBP <90 or DBP ≤60), Age ≥65
- Score 0-1: Outpatient, Score 2: Short stay/Observation, Score 3-5: ICU

**Empiric Treatment (non-severe):**
- Amoxicillin 1g TID OR Doxycycline OR Macrolide (if local resistance <25%)

**COVID-19 Treatment:**
- Outpatient (high risk): Paxlovid (nirmatrelvir/ritonavir) within 5 days
- Hospitalized (requiring O2): Remdesivir + Dexamethasone
- Hospitalized (NOT requiring O2): Remdesivir

### Gastrointestinal Infections

**Traveler's Diarrhea:**
- Mild: Loperamide + Oral rehydration
- Moderate: Azithromycin 500mg daily x3 days OR Rifaximin
- Severe/dysentery: Azithromycin 500mg daily x3 days (avoid fluoroquinolones due to resistance)

**C. difficile Infection:**
- First episode: Fidaxomicin 200mg PO BID x10 days (preferred) OR Vancomycin 125mg PO QID x10 days
- Recurrent: Fidaxomicin standard or extended regimen, consider fecal transplant after 2nd recurrence
- **NEVER**: Loperamide, metronidazole (no longer recommended as first line)

### Sexually Transmitted Infections

**Syphilis:**
- Primary/secondary: Benzathine penicillin G 2.4 million units IM single dose
- Tertiary/late latent: Benzathine penicillin G 2.4 million units IM weekly x3
- Neurosyphilis: Aqueous penicillin G 3-4 million units IV q4h x10-14 days

**Gonorrhea:**
- Ceftriaxone 500mg IM single dose (1g if ≥150kg)
- Add doxycycline 100mg BID x7 days if chlamydia not excluded

**Chlamydia:**
- Doxycycline 100mg BID x7 days OR Azithromycin 1g single dose (pregnant: azithromycin)

**HIV (PrEP/PEP):**
- PrEP: Tenofovir DF/Emtricitabine daily OR Tenofovir AF/Emtricitabine (Descovy, preferred)
- PEP (within 72h of exposure): Tenofovir/Emtricitabine + Raltegravir x28 days

### Tick-Borne Diseases

**Lyme Disease:**
- Erythema migrans: Doxycycline 100mg BID x10 days OR Amoxicillin 500mg TID x14 days
- Neurologic: Ceftriaxone 2g IV daily x14-21 days

**Rocky Mountain Spotted Fever:**
- Doxycycline 100mg BID x5-7 days (don't wait for rash)

## Antimicrobial Reference

### Gram-Positive Coverage
- **MSSA**: Cefazolin, Nafcillin/Oxacillin, Dicloxacillin
- **MRSA**: Vancomycin, Daptomycin, Linezolid, TMP-SMX, Clindamycin, Doxycycline
- **VRE**: Linezolid, Daptomycin (NOT pneumonia), Fosfomycin (UTI only)

### Gram-Negative Coverage
- **Pseudomonas**: Piperacillin-tazobactam, Cefepime, Ceftazidime, Meropenem, Aztreonam, Ciprofloxacin, Levofloxacin, Amikacin, Tobramycin
- **ESBL**: Carbapenems (Ertapenem, Meropenem), Cefiderocol
- **CRE**: Meropenem-vaborbactam, Cefiderocol, TMP-SMX, Fosfomycin, Aminoglycosides
- **Anaerobes**: Metronidazole, Clindamycin, Piperacillin-tazobactam, Carbapenems, Ampicillin-sulbactam

### Antifungal Spectrum
- **Candida**: Fluconazole (susceptible), Echinocandins (Micafungin, Caspofungin), Amphotericin B
- **Aspergillus**: Voriconazole (preferred), Isavuconazole, Amphotericin B
- **Mucor/Rhizopus**: Amphotericin B (liposomal preferred) + Surgery
- **Cryptococcus**: Amphotericin B + Flucytosine (induction), Fluconazole (consolidation/maintenance)
- **Histoplasma/Coccidioides**: Itraconazole or Fluconazole (mild), Amphotericin B (severe)

### Antiviral Reference
- **HSV/VZV**: Acyclovir, Valacyclovir
- **CMV**: Ganciclovir, Valganciclovir, Foscarnet
- **Influenza**: Oseltamivir (Tamiflu), Baloxavir (preferred if within 48h)
- **Hepatitis B**: Tenofovir, Entecavir
- **Hepatitis C**: Direct-acting antivirals (Sofosbuvir/Velpatasvir)

## Infection Control & Isolation

### Standard Precautions
- Hand hygiene (alcohol-based preferred)
- PPE when risk of splash/contact
- Safe injection practices
- Respiratory hygiene/cough etiquette

### Transmission-Based Precautions
- **Contact**: MRSA, VRE, C. difficile, RSV, draining wounds
  - Gown + gloves, dedicated equipment
  - C. difficile: Soap and water handwashing (alcohol doesn't kill spores)
- **Droplet**: Influenza, Pertussis, Meningococcus, Adenovirus
  - Surgical mask within 3 feet
- **Airborne**: TB, Measles, Varicella, SARS-CoV-2 (in some contexts)
  - N95 respirator or PAPR, negative pressure room

## Vaccination Schedule (Adult Key)

- **Influenza**: Annually
- **Tdap**: Every 10 years (one Tdap, then Td)
- **HPV**: Through age 26 (3 doses if starting >15 years)
- **Pneumococcal**: PCV13 or PCV20 once + PPSV23 at ≥65 (if PCV20 not used)
- **Hepatitis B**: 2 or 3 dose series
- **Shingles (Zoster)**: Recombinant vaccine (Shingrix) 2 doses at ≥50
- **COVID-19**: Primary series + boosters per current recommendations

## Key Lab Values & Interpretation

### Inflammatory Markers
- **Procalcitonin**: <0.25 = bacterial unlikely, >0.5 = suggestive, >2 = strongly suggestive of bacterial infection/sepsis
- **CRP**: Non-specific, rises in 4-6h, peaks 36-50h
- **ESR**: Non-specific, influenced by anemia, age, pregnancy

### Culture Interpretation
- **Blood cultures**: 2 sets (aerobic + anaerobic each) from 2 different sites
  - Single bottle positive: Contamination likely (unless S. aureus, gram negatives, yeast, or Bacteroides)
  - Both bottles positive: True bacteremia
- **Urine culture**: >10^5 CFU/mL = significant bacteriuria (with symptoms = UTI)
- **Sputum culture**: Only if Gram stain shows <10 epithelial cells and >25 PMNs per LPF

## Antimicrobial Stewardship Principles

1. **Obtain cultures BEFORE starting antibiotics** (when stable)
2. **Narrow/de-escalate** when susceptibilities return
3. **Oral switch** when clinically improving and able to tolerate PO
4. **Duration optimization**: Shortest effective (e.g., 5 days for uncomplicated pneumonia)
5. **Document indication** and planned duration at initiation
6. **Review daily** for necessity and de-escalation opportunities
